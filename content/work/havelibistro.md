## Haveli Bistro

When an upscale dining establishment enters a competitive downtown market like Columbus, Ohio, success hinges on immediate visibility and brand cohesion. For Haveli Bistro, We directed the digital market entry, visual identity, and online discovery strategy to position the new venue as a premier local culinary destination.


### The Objective

Launching a physical restaurant requires synchronizing multiple customer touchpoints simultaneously. Haveli Bistro needed a distinct visual presence, print-to-digital design alignment, and a localized marketing framework to capture foot traffic and search volume from day one.

### Strategic Execution

#### Brand Identity & Asset Design

Before establishing an online presence, I built the visual foundation of the restaurant. This involved translating the "upscale bistro" concept into a cohesive set of assets used across both digital platforms and physical operations.

- **Visual Structure**: Developed a modern color palette and typography system tailored for a premium hospitality brand.
- **Collateral Design**: Created high-resolution, readable layouts for physical menus, signage, and digital promotional materials, maintaining strict brand consistency.
- **Content Strategy**: Crafted messaging that highlighted the restaurant’s unique culinary offerings, ensuring the tone matched the premium dining experience.

#### Website Design & User Experience

The website was designed as a high-conversion marketing asset, built specifically to handle rapid menu browsing and immediate action.

- **Mobile-First Layout**: Optimized the interface layout primarily for smartphones, ensuring that menus, hours, and reservation links are accessible within a single tap.
- **Operational Autonomy**: Deployed the site on a reliable, low-overhead platform, allowing management to update daily specials or adjust hours in real time without technical bottlenecks.

#### Digital Marketing & Local Search Optimization

A beautiful brand is useless if local diners cannot find it. We implemented a targeted local marketing layer to dominate search intent in the downtown Columbus area.

**Local Search Strategy**: Structured the website content to rank for high-intent search terms like "upscale dining Columbus" and specific regional cuisine keywords.

**Map & Discovery Integration**: Fully integrated and optimized local business profiles to capture high-volume "near me" traffic, converting search engine impressions into physical foot traffic.

Business Impact

By focusing on a unified brand launch rather than disjointed marketing pieces, Haveli Bistro opened with a mature market footprint. The combination of a clear visual identity, frictionless mobile browsing, and a deliberate local search strategy gave the restaurant the visibility needed to build an immediate customer base in a crowded downtown grid.
